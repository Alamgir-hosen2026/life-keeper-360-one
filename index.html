1. Overview of the App

Life Keeper 360 One is an all-in-one personal life management application for Android. It is designed to help you organize your personal profile, important dates, reminders, documents, finances, home information, vehicle records, personal assets, notes, and reports, all in one place. The App includes the following modules:

  • Personal Profile
  • Life Dashboard
  • Important Dates
  • Smart Reminders
  • Document Keeper
  • Personal Finance
  • Home Information
  • Vehicle Information
  • Personal Asset Register
  • Personal Notes
  • Reports
  • Privacy & Security
  • Backup Data (Export / Import / Restore)
  • PDF Export
  • Settings

Access to the App's full feature set requires an active yearly subscription, managed through Google Play Billing. Every module listed above stores its information only in a local database on your device.

3. Data Collected by Each Feature

3.1 Photos and Attachments

When you attach a photo to your Profile, a Document, or an Asset, the App accesses your device's photo gallery only at the moment you choose an image. The App does not browse, scan, or upload your gallery in the background. The selected image reference is stored locally so it can be displayed within the App.

3.2 Life Dashboard and Reports

The Dashboard and Reports modules read data already stored in the App's local database (such as upcoming deadlines, expiring documents, payments, and reminders) to display summaries. No new data is collected — these features only organize and present information you have already entered.

3.3 PDF Export

The PDF Export feature generates PDF documents (Personal Summary, Asset Report, Vehicle Report, Document List, Expense Report) directly on your device using the data stored locally. These PDF files are saved to your device and are only shared if you choose to share or save them yourself through Android's share options.

5. Local Storage — No Server, No Cloud

Life Keeper 360 One does not use a remote server, cloud database, or any Developer-operated backend to store your personal information. All data you enter is saved directly on your device using a local database. If you uninstall the App, or clear its data, this locally stored information is permanently deleted, unless you have previously created a backup file as described below.

6. Backup, Export & Restore (.lkb files)

The Backup Data module lets you create a single backup file (with the .lkb extension) that packages all of your data from every module, along with any attached photos, into one file. This process happens entirely on your device:

  • When you tap Backup Data, the App builds the .lkb file locally and then opens the Android Share Sheet, letting you decide where to save it — for example Google Drive, WhatsApp, email, or your device's own storage. The Developer does not receive a copy of this file.
  • When you tap Restore Data, you select a previously created .lkb file from your device, and the App reads it locally to restore your information. Restoring replaces the data currently in the App with the data from the backup file.

Because backup files may contain sensitive personal information (documents, financial details, photos), you are responsible for storing and sharing your backup files securely.

7. Subscription & Payment Information

Full access to Life Keeper 360 One requires a yearly subscription, processed through Google Play Billing. When you subscribe:

  • Your payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The App and the Developer do not receive, process, or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial account information.
  • Google may share limited purchase information (such as subscription status and purchase token) with the Developer solely to verify that your subscription is active, in accordance with Google Play's own policies.
  • For details on how Google handles your payment data, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

8. Security Features (PIN, Fingerprint, Auto-Lock)

  • PIN: If you set a PIN, it is converted into a one-way cryptographic hash before being saved to your device. The App never stores your PIN in plain, readable text, and the Developer has no way to view or recover it.
  • Fingerprint Unlock: If enabled, biometric authentication is performed entirely by your device's built-in Android biometric system. The App only receives a success or failure result — it never accesses, transmits, or stores fingerprint data itself.
  • Auto-Lock: When enabled, the App requires your PIN or fingerprint again after being left in the background, to prevent unauthorized access if someone else picks up your device.
  • Hide Sensitive Information: When enabled, the App prevents screenshots and hides its content in the Android Recent Apps screen.

10. Notifications & Reminders

Smart Reminder notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android's local alarm and notification system. No reminder content is sent to or processed by any external server.

11. Children's Privacy

Life Keeper 360 One is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. As all data is stored locally and no data is transmitted to us, we have no visibility into who uses the App on a given device. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this App, please have the device owner delete that information directly within the App.

12. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data remains on your device for as long as the App is installed and its data is not cleared.
  • You can delete individual entries (documents, notes, finance records, etc.) at any time from within each module.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage/data from Android Settings, permanently deletes all locally stored information, unless you have saved a backup .lkb file elsewhere.
  • Because we do not operate a server, we have no copy of your data to delete on our end — deleting data within the App, or uninstalling it, is the complete and final deletion of that information.

13. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & Portability: You can view all your data at any time within the App, and export a complete copy at any time using Backup Data.
  • Correction: You can edit or update any entry directly within the relevant module.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual entries, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • Permissions: You can review and revoke any Android permission granted to the App at any time through your device's Settings ▶ Apps ▶ Life Keeper 360 One ▶ Permissions.

14. How We Protect Your Data

Because your data is stored only on your own device rather than on a server we control, the primary responsibility for protecting it rests with standard Android app sandboxing and any device-level protections you use (device lock screen, encryption). Within the App itself, we additionally provide:

  • Optional PIN and fingerprint app-lock
  • Optional auto-lock after backgrounding
  • Optional hiding of app content from screenshots and the Recent Apps screen
  • PINs stored only as irreversible hashes, never in plain text

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device's operating system up to date and to use a secure device lock screen.
